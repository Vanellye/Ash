# Ash Knowledge Orchard — Creative Seed

## Story — The Orchard of Small Truths
An orchard keeper planted every claim in a separate pot. Some seeds grew quickly; some did not sprout; some turned out to be stones. The keeper labeled each pot with who brought it, when it arrived, and how much sunlight it had received. Visitors were free to admire the orchard, but no one could call a seed a tree before it grew.

## Art direction — Knowledge garden
A quiet botanical plate: five pots labeled OBSERVATION, INTERPRETATION, HYPOTHESIS, EVIDENCE, and OPEN QUESTION. Roots are visible through glass; no plant is shown as mature unless its review state supports that status.

## Music sketch — “Five Pots” (original)
Five short motifs, each four notes long: C–E–G–E; D–F–A–F; E–G–B–G; F–A–C–A; G–B–D–B. Play each with a different timbre, then layer them softly without collapsing their identities.

## Curation rules
Keep observations separate from interpretations and hypotheses. Attach provenance, confidence, sensitivity classification, and reviewer notes. A transfer receipt is not evidence approval; publication requires explicit human authorization.
